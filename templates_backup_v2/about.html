About Us

Vinayaka Chavithi

Vinayaka Chavithi is one of the most important Hindu festivals dedicated to Lord Ganesha. It is celebrated with devotion, prayers, cultural events, and community gatherings.

GMP TIGERS

GMP TIGERS proudly organizes the Vinayaka Chavithi celebrations in Godugumani Palle. Our mission is to bring the community together through devotion, culture, and service.

Festival Activities

• Daily Pooja
• Cultural Programs
• Annadanam
• Bhajans
• Grand Vinayaka Procession
• Community Celebrations
