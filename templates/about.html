🙏 About GMP TIGERS

Serving the community through devotion, unity and culture.

Our Mission

GMP TIGERS is a community organization dedicated to celebrating Vinayaka Chavithi with devotion, cultural programs, social service, and unity among the people of Godugumani Palle.

Our Vision

To preserve traditions, encourage youth participation, and organize a memorable festival every year while serving the community with dedication.
