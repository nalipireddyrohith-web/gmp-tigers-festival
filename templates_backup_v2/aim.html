Our Aim

🎯 Our Aim

To organize a peaceful, devotional, and memorable Vinayaka Chavithi celebration that brings together the people of Godugumani Palle through faith, culture, and unity.

🌟 Our Mission

To preserve traditions, encourage youth participation, promote cultural activities, and serve the community through devotion and social responsibility.

🚀 Our Vision

To make GMP TIGERS Vinayaka Chavithi celebrations a symbol of unity, devotion, cultural heritage, and community service for future generations.
